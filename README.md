An entry for the make.fun bounty **"The Best Meal You Ever Had"**: one
sentence, at most 200 characters, no title and no list of courses — a single
sentence that has to make the meal feel concrete, personal and worth
remembering.

The sentence:

> My grandmother's rendang, eaten cross-legged on her kitchen floor at
> midnight, her pretending not to see me reach for a third helping.

Nothing in it is decoration. The rendang names the dish; the kitchen floor
and the midnight hour place it outside every restaurant on earth; and the
last clause is the meal's real flavour — a grandmother who has cooked all
day and now performs not noticing, because being caught taking a third
helping would end the game both of them are playing. The best meal is never
just the food, and the sentence refuses to pretend otherwise.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies, no network) clears the contents of the
output directory and writes the sentence as UTF-8 text to `entry.txt`
inside it.
